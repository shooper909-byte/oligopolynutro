1. Agreement and program purpose

By applying to, enrolling in, or participating in the OligoPoly Research Partner Program (the "Program"), the applicant or approved partner ("Partner") agrees to these terms. The Program permits approved Partners to refer qualified research-market customers to OligoPoly Laboratories ("OligoPoly") using authorized tracking links, codes and creative. Participation does not create employment, agency, franchise, joint venture or authority to bind OligoPoly.

2. Eligibility and approval

  • Partner must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter this agreement.
  • Partner must provide accurate identity, contact, channel, audience, tax and payment information.
  • Approval is discretionary, channel-specific and revocable. OligoPoly may reject or remove applicants without an obligation to provide a reason.
  • One person or business may not operate duplicate accounts without written approval.
  • Partner must not participate from a jurisdiction or through a channel where the Program, products or promotion would be unlawful or prohibited.

3. Approved promotion

Partner may use only current OligoPoly-approved links, codes, images and claims. All promotion must clearly position products for research use only, follow the Partner Compliance Rules, and comply with applicable laws, advertising rules, platform terms and disclosure requirements. OligoPoly may require correction or removal of content at any time.

4. Commission and eligible revenue

The pilot commission is 10% of Net Eligible Product Revenue unless a written offer in the partner portal states otherwise. "Net Eligible Product Revenue" means the collected product subtotal for eligible products, after discounts, refunds, credits and chargebacks, excluding shipping, taxes, duties, tips, donations, gift-card value, payment fees and other non-product amounts.

No commission is earned on cancelled, refunded, returned, fraudulent, disputed, test or self-referred orders; orders violating these terms; wholesale or specially priced orders unless approved; or purchases OligoPoly cannot reliably attribute. OligoPoly may reverse previously posted commission when an order later becomes ineligible.

5. Attribution

The standard attribution window is 60 days from the customer's last eligible affiliate click. A valid, authorized partner code entered at checkout may receive attribution when the platform records that code, unless the order is otherwise ineligible. Where a qualifying click and a qualifying code conflict, the recorded code controls; otherwise, the most recent eligible affiliate click controls. Browser restrictions, deleted cookies, device switching and blocked tracking can prevent attribution. OligoPoly's good-faith platform records govern.

6. Payouts, threshold and taxes

  • Approved commissions are calculated monthly after a 30-day validation hold and paid on the Program's published schedule.
  • The minimum payout is $100. Smaller balances roll forward until the threshold is reached.
  • Partner is responsible for accurate payout details, taxes, reporting and required tax forms. OligoPoly may withhold payment until required documentation is complete.
  • Partner must report a suspected tracking or payout error within 30 days of the applicable statement. OligoPoly may correct clerical or platform errors.

Sensitive tax and payment details are collected only through the secure partner platform after approval — never through the public application form.

  • False or unsupported claims about quality, testing, certification, approval, scarcity, results, reviews or endorsements.
