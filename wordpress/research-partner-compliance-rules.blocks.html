These Partner Compliance Rules ("Rules") apply to every OligoPoly Research Partner, every promotional channel, and every piece of content that references OligoPoly Laboratories or its products. Violations may result in content-removal requests, commission reversal, or immediate removal from the Program.

1. Research use only — always

  • All OligoPoly products are for laboratory research use only. Every promotion must make this positioning clear and must never state or imply otherwise.
  • No human-use implications of any kind — direct, indirect, implied by imagery, testimonial, anecdote, hashtag, or context.
  • No medical, therapeutic, treatment, disease, safety, or efficacy claims.
  • No dosing guidance, no reconstitution-for-human-use content, no injection or administration content, and no self-experimentation content.

2. Honest, disclosed promotion

  • Place a clear, conspicuous affiliate disclosure near every endorsement or referral link (for example: "Affiliate link — I may earn a commission"), consistent with FTC guidance and the rules of each platform you use.
  • Use only approved OligoPoly facts, product imagery, and promotional materials. Do not create, alter, or imply product claims beyond the approved language.
  • Never present OligoPoly content as independent editorial when it is compensated.

3. Prohibited tactics

  • No bidding on OligoPoly trademarks, brand names, or confusingly similar terms in paid search.
  • No cookie stuffing, forced clicks, iframe or redirect tricks, or any manipulation of attribution.
  • No self-referrals — partners may not earn commission on their own purchases or purchases they direct.
  • No scraping, publishing, or promoting coupon codes that OligoPoly has not issued to you, and no unauthorized coupon or "deal" promotion.
  • No spam, unsolicited bulk messaging, incentivized traffic, or misleading urgency and scarcity tactics.
  • Paid advertising of any kind requires prior written approval from OligoPoly.

4. Audience and channel standards

  • Promote only to audiences plausibly composed of researchers, laboratory professionals, and legitimate research communities.
  • Do not target consumers seeking health, fitness, longevity, or self-treatment outcomes.
  • Register every promotional channel with OligoPoly; promoting on unregistered channels requires prior approval.

5. Corrections and enforcement

  • Partners must correct or remove any content promptly when OligoPoly requests it — typically within 48 hours of notice.
  • OligoPoly monitors partner content and may reverse commissions tied to non-compliant promotion.
  • Serious or repeated violations result in immediate removal from the Program under the Program Terms.
