DRAFT — PENDING LEGAL REVIEW. These Rules form part of the Research Partner Program Terms. Publish only after counsel review.

These rules are mandatory. They apply to every caption, video, article, email, link, code, image, live stream, comment and private-community post.

The five non-negotiables

  1. Research use only must be clear. Do not imply personal, clinical, veterinary, diagnostic or therapeutic use.
  2. No medical or human-outcome claims. Do not describe treatment, prevention, cure, weight loss, recovery, performance, safety, efficacy or expected bodily results.
  3. No dosing or administration content. Do not publish reconstitution for human use, schedules, routes, injection content, stacks or self-experimentation protocols.
  4. Disclose the relationship clearly. Put a plain-language affiliate disclosure near the endorsement or link — not only in a profile, footer or hashtag block.
  5. Use approved facts and assets. Do not invent testing, purity, certifications, approvals, testimonials, studies, scarcity or guarantees.

Disclosure examples

  • Short post: "Affiliate link: I may earn a commission if you purchase through this link."
  • Video: State the relationship verbally near the endorsement and place the same disclosure on screen and in the description before the links.
  • Article or newsletter: Place "This page contains affiliate links. I may earn a commission from qualifying purchases." before the first affiliate link.
  • Discount code: "OligoPoly partner code. I may receive a commission when you use it."

Content review checklist

  • The research-use-only context is explicit and prominent.
  • No person is shown consuming, injecting, dosing or implying use.
  • No disease, treatment, therapeutic, safety, efficacy or performance outcome appears.
  • Scientific statements are accurate, sourced when material, and do not become product-outcome claims.
  • Affiliate disclosure is clear, proximate and understandable.
  • Link, code, price and promotion details are current.
  • Only approved logos, images and product descriptions are used.
  • The content follows the host platform's current rules.
