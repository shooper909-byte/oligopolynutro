Research integrity comes first. Partners must disclose the affiliate relationship, keep research-use-only positioning clear, and avoid medical, therapeutic, human-use, safety, efficacy, dosing, and administration claims. OligoPoly reviews partner channels and may require corrections or end participation for violations. See the complete Partner Compliance Rules.

  • All OligoPoly products are for laboratory research use only — every promotion must make this clear.
  • No human-use implications of any kind, direct or indirect.

Frequently asked questions

The essentials of how the Research Partner Program works.

What commission rate does the program pay?

The pilot rate is 10% of net eligible product revenue, unless a written offer in the partner portal states otherwise. Earnings are not guaranteed and depend on validated attributable purchases.

What counts as eligible revenue?

Net Eligible Product Revenue is the collected product subtotal for eligible products, after discounts, refunds, credits, and chargebacks — excluding shipping, taxes, duties, tips, donations, gift-card value, payment fees, and other non-product amounts. No commission is earned on cancelled, refunded, returned, fraudulent, disputed, test, or self-referred orders.

How long is the attribution window?

60 days from the customer’s last eligible affiliate click. A valid, authorized partner code entered at checkout may also receive attribution. Where a qualifying click and a qualifying code conflict, the recorded code controls; otherwise the most recent eligible click controls. Browser restrictions, deleted cookies, and blocked tracking can prevent attribution.

When are commissions paid?

Approved commissions are calculated monthly after a 30-day validation hold and paid on the Program’s published schedule, once the $100 minimum is reached. Smaller balances roll forward. Report a suspected tracking or payout error within 30 days of the applicable statement.

What happens with refunds or chargebacks?

The order becomes ineligible and the associated commission may be reversed — including commission already posted. That is what the 30-day validation hold is for.

Can I run paid advertising?

Only with prior written approval. Paid search on OligoPoly trademarks or confusing variants, direct-link ads, and policy evasion are prohibited. Any approved paid activity follows the same research-use-only and disclosure rules as organic content.

Do I need to disclose my affiliate relationship?

Yes. Each endorsement or affiliate link needs a clear, plain-language disclosure placed near it — not only in a profile, footer, or hashtag block.

What content is prohibited?

Human-use, dosing, administration, and medical-outcome content — including reconstitution for human use, schedules, routes, injection content, stacks, and self-experimentation protocols. Also prohibited: treatment, disease, therapeutic, safety, and efficacy claims. OligoPoly products are for laboratory research use only.

Is approval automatic?

No. Enrollment is selective and approval is discretionary. Every application is reviewed manually, and the pilot cohort is limited to 10–20 partners. Links and codes are issued only after approval and a recorded compliance acknowledgment.

Can applications be rejected or partners removed?

Yes. OligoPoly may reject or remove applicants without an obligation to provide a reason. Material or repeated violations may result in immediate termination and reversal of commissions connected to noncompliant activity, as described in the Program Terms.
