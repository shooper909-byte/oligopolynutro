Frequently asked questions

The essentials of how the Research Partner Program works.

What commission rate does the program pay?

The pilot pays a 10% commission on net eligible product revenue from validated referred purchases. Rates may be reviewed when the 90-day pilot concludes.

What counts as eligible revenue?

Eligible revenue is the net product subtotal of qualifying referred orders — excluding shipping, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, canceled orders, and purchases excluded under the Program Terms (for example self-referrals or orders using unauthorized discounts).

How long is the attribution window?

60 days from the last qualifying click on your tracked referral link. If a customer purchases within that window, the order is attributed to you.

When are commissions paid?

Payouts are issued monthly after a 30-day validation hold on each order. The minimum payout is $100; smaller balances roll forward to the next cycle.

What happens with refunds or chargebacks?

Commissions on refunded, charged-back, or canceled orders are reversed. That is what the 30-day validation hold is for — it keeps payouts accurate for everyone.

Can I run paid advertising?

Paid placements require prior written approval. Bidding on OligoPoly trademarks or brand terms is prohibited, and any approved paid activity must follow the same research-use-only and disclosure rules as organic content.

Do I need to disclose my affiliate relationship?

Yes. A clear, conspicuous affiliate disclosure must appear near every endorsement or referral link, consistent with FTC guidance and platform rules.

What content is prohibited?

Anything implying human use: dosing guidance, reconstitution for human use, injection or administration content, treatment or disease claims, therapeutic or safety and efficacy claims, and self-experimentation content. OligoPoly products are for laboratory research use only.

Is approval automatic?

No. Every application is reviewed manually by our partnerships team, and the pilot cohort is limited to 10–20 approved partners. Referral links, codes, and accounts are only issued after approval.

Can applications be rejected or partners removed?

Yes. We may decline any application at our discretion, and partners who violate the Compliance Rules can be removed from the program, with unvalidated commissions forfeited as described in the Program Terms.
